title: "Bugtracker has been relocated from SourceForge to openmediavault.org"
date: 2012-06-27T22:14:13
slug: "bugtracker-has-been-relocated-to-openmediavault-org"
categories: ["News"]
---

Because SourceForge is discontinuing their hosted apps the bugtracker has been relocated to the openmediavault.org home. The existing database has been reimported, thus all existing data still exists. Already registered users must request a new password to be able to login. In most cases you must use your SourceForge email address, e.g. <username>@users.sourceforge.net, as recipient. The new bugtracker will be available after a short downtime.
