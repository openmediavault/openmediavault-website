The S.M.A.R.T. management has been finished now. It is possible to [view](http://sourceforge.net/apps/gallery/openmediavault/index.php?g2_itemId=49) and configure various S.M.A.R.T. [parameters](http://sourceforge.net/apps/gallery/openmediavault/index.php?g2_itemId=130) like the scan interval, temperature monitoring and scheduled tests. If the email notification is enabled the user will be notified in case of a S.M.A.R.T. error.
