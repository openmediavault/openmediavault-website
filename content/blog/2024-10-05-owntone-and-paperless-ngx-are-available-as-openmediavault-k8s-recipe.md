Two new recipes for the [owntone](https://owntone.github.io/owntone-server/) and [paperless-ngx](https://docs.paperless-ngx.com/) apps have been released to the [openmediavault-k8s-recipes GitHub repository](https://github.com/openmediavault/openmediavault-k8s-recipes). You can get them after updating the recipes list in your openmediavault-k8s plugin.

The following list of applications is now available:

- filebrowser
- immich
- jellyfin
- minio
- owntone
- paperless-ngx
- photoprism
- plex-media-server
- wetty

Contributions from the community are welcome to increase the number of recipes.
