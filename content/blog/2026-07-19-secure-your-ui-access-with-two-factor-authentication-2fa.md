Did you know there are now 2 new plugins for two-factor authentication in openmediavault?

- **openmediavault-2fa-totp** – Time-based one-time passwords, compatible with Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator, OTP Auth and more.
- **openmediavault-2fa-emailotp** – Prefer no extra app? Just get your one-time password via email straight to your inbox.

Both work for all user accounts, including admin. No excuses not to secure your NAS!
