today the openmediavault project announces the special operation ‘FalseNAS’ where it will switch to FreeBSD with GNU Hurd kernel in version 7.

The main reason behind this switch is to have support of the breaking ground new APFS file system. Another killer feature will be the support of Google Android apps. This will allow you to install and use them directly on your NAS. Because of the massiv changes we expect OMV7 not before 01.04.2029.
