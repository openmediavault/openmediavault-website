**openmediavault 6.0.8**

## WARNING … This update will break side-by-side installations with graphical desktops.

- Various improvements.
- Update locale files.
- Add the ability to copy and apply the permissions of a shared folder to its ACL page.
- Add ‘Conflicts’ section in package control; openmediavault can’t be installed side-by-side with graphical environments anymore.
