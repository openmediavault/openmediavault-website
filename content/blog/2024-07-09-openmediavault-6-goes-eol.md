title: "openmediavault 6 approaching EOL"
date: 2024-07-09T14:21:24
slug: "openmediavault-6-goes-eol"
categories: ["News"]
---

At 31.07.2024 openmediavault 6.x will become EOL because Debian 11 [estimates](https://wiki.debian.org/DebianReleases) EOL (end-of-life) for the same time. This means no security/bugfix updates will be released anymore. Please upgrade to 7.x to be up-to-date.
