Three new recipes for the openmediavault-k8s plugin have been added:

- ACME DNS
- Let’s Encrypt
- Uptime Kuma

Contributions from the community are welcome to increase the number of recipes. See <https://github.com/openmediavault/openmediavault-k8s-recipes> for more information.
