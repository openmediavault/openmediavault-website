At 10.06.2026 openmediavault 7 will become EOL because Debian 12 [estimates](https://www.debian.org/releases/) EOL (end-of-life) for the same time. This means no security/bugfix updates will be released anymore. Please upgrade to openmediavault 8 to be up-to-date.
