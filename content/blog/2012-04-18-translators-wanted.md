Starting with OpenMediaVault 0.3 the WebGUI supports internationalization (i18n). Because of that translators are wanted helping to support as much languages as possible. To become a translator you have to do the following:

- Subscribe the OMV contributor license agreement. You can download it [here](/contributoragreement.html).
- Create an account at Transifex, the service used for collaborative translation. You can create it [here](http://translate.openmediavault.org).
- After your account has been enabled you can start translating.
