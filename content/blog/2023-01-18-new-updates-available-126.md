**openmediavault 6.1.6**

- Update locales.
- Issue #1432: Add support to customize the notification emails via environment variables. The environment variable OMV_NUT_UPSSCHEDCMD_EMAIL_SUBJECT supports the placeholders %d (date) and %h (hostname).
