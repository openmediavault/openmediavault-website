With the release of openmediavault 8.5.0, an [authentication framework for the UI](https://github.com/openmediavault/openmediavault/blob/master/deb/openmediavault/usr/share/openmediavault/docs/AUTH_PLUGIN_GUIDE.md) was introduced. This allows plugins to implement a wide variety of two-step multi-factor authentication methods. The [2FA TOTP plugin](https://github.com/openmediavault/openmediavault/tree/master/deb/openmediavault-2fa-totp) is the first of these.
