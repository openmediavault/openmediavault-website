In addition to the [plugins](/plugins.html), the system can also be enhanced via containerized/dockerized applications. You can find the app catalog [here](https://github.com/openmediavault/openmediavault-k8s-recipes). To make use of the apps, you need to install the *openmediavault-k8s* Kubernetes plugin.
